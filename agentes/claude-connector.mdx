# Tupana connector for Claude

[Tupana](https://www.tupana.ai) is a Chilean electronic invoicing platform. The Tupana connector lets Claude work with your companies' data in Tupana: search and summarize documents, prepare invoices and credit notes, follow up on unpaid invoices, reconcile bank movements and read tax summaries from the SII (Chile's tax authority).

## Connection details

| | |
|---|---|
| Server URL | `https://mcp.tupana.ai/mcp` |
| Transport | Streamable HTTP (remote MCP) |
| Authentication | OAuth 2.1 with PKCE and dynamic client registration |
| Sign-in | Your Tupana account (email and password, or Google) |
| API key | Not needed |

## Setup

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** and add the Tupana connector (or **Add custom connector** with the URL above).
2. Sign in with your Tupana account when the browser opens.
3. Ask Claude about your invoices, clients or bank movements.

Claude Code: `claude mcp add --transport http tupana https://mcp.tupana.ai/mcp`, then run `/mcp` and choose `tupana` to sign in.

## How issuing works

Issuing a tax document in Chile is legally binding and cannot be deleted (only cancelled with a credit note), so every issuing flow has a review step:

1. **Draft**: `create_sale_invoice`, `create_credit_note`, `create_purchase_invoice` and `create_export_invoice` create a draft. A draft has no folio (document number) and is not sent to the SII.
2. **Preview**: `get_document_preview` renders the draft as a PDF.
3. **Issue**: `emit_document` sends the draft to the SII. It refuses to issue a draft that has no preview.

Recurring invoices created with `create_scheduled_invoice` start **inactive**: nothing is issued until you activate them in Tupana.

Each tool declares whether it is read-only or writes data. Claude asks for your confirmation before running the tools that issue documents, send email or record a reconciliation.

## Permissions and data

- Claude acts with **your** Tupana permissions and only sees the companies you belong to.
- The connector returns only the data needed for each request, paginated (at most 100 rows per page).
- The connector does not read your conversation history or files.
- Your data is processed under the [Tupana privacy policy](https://www.tupana.ai/privacy). Security practices: [tupana.ai/seguridad](https://www.tupana.ai/seguridad).
- To revoke access, disconnect the connector in Claude.

## What it does not do

- It does not do bookkeeping (journal entries, ledger, balance sheet).
- It does not issue dispatch guides (DTE 52) or bulk uploads; those are available in the Tupana app and the [REST API](/api-reference/v2/introduction).
- It does not move money or make payments.
